# Md. Khairul Bashar Bhuiyan

Research Assistant | Machine Learning | Quantum Computing | Cloud Computing

---

Md. Khairul Bashar Bhuiyan is an undergraduate research assistant at the Control Application and Research Center (CARC). He has received a Bachelor’s degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Brac University and a Master’s degree from the Royal University of Dhaka(RUD). He has previously worked on projects related to a satellite training kit and a lie-detector polygraph machine. He is currently working on projects related to Drone communication and Remote sensing. He is interested in research fields such as Machine learning, IoT and satellite communication.
